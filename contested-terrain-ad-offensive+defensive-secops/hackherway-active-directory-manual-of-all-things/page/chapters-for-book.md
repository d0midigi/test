**PART X: HACKING, ATTACKING, AND SECURING NETWORK OPERATING SYSTEMS AND INFRASTRUCTURES: ETHICAL HACKING AND COUNTERMEASURES: OFFENSIVE AND DEFENSIVE CYBERSECURITY**

Part X: _Hacking, Attacking, and Securing Network Operating Systems and Infrastructures: Ethical Hacking and Countermeasures: Offensive and Defensive Cybersecurity_ is crafted for individuals aspiring to become security officers, auditory, security specialists, vulnerability assessment and management specialists, site administrators, or anyone tasked with ensuring the security and integrity of information systems, data, and network infrastructures. This part covers a wide array of topics within offensive hacks and attacks against network security, ethical hacking, and defensive strategies and countermeasures. Designed to plunge learners into an engaging learning experience, this part teaches how to perform scans, tests, hacks, and secure information systems. It introduces a spectrum of tools, viruses, and malware, while offering a holistic view of hacker tactics, techniques, procedures (TTPs), and tools. Through a deep comprehension of hacker methodologies, ethical hackers will be equipped to develop resilient countermeasures and protective systems for safeguarding their organization’s most critical assets and information. Completing this part of the handbook will equip readers with the knowledge necessary to successfully pursue their attainable goal of becoming a hacker in cybersecurity today.
