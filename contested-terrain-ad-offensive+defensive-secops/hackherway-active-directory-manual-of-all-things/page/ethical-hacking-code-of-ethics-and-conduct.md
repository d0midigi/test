# Ethical Hacking Code of Ethics and Conduct

Ethical Hacking Code of Ethics and Conduct

This code of ethics expresses the consensus of the profession on ethical issues and is a means to educate both the public and those who are entering the field about the ethical obligations of all e-commerce consultants. It is expected that each ethical hacker abides by the following standards of ethics:

1. Keep private any confidential information gained in her/his professional work, in particular as it pertains to client lists and client personal information. Not collect, give, sell, or transfer any personal information (such as name, email address, Social Security Number, or other unique identifiers to a third-party without prior client consent.
2. Protect the intellectual properties of others by relying on her/his own innovation and efforts, thus ensuring that all benefits vest with its originator.
3. Disclose to appropriate persons or authorities the potential dangers to any e-commerce clients, the Internet communities, or the public, that she/he reasonably believes to be associated with a particular set or type of electronic transactions or related software or hardware.
4. Provide service in their areas of competence, being honest and forthright about any limitations of her/his experience and education. Ensure that she/he is qualified for any project on which she/he works or proposes to work by an appropriate combination of education, training, and experience.
5. Never knowingly use software or process information and data that is obligated or retained either illegally or unethically.
6. Not engage in deceptive financial practices such as bribery, double billing, or other improper financial practices.
7. Use the properties of a client or employer only in ways properly authorized, and with the owner’s knowledge and consent.
8. Disclose to all concerned parties those conflicts of interest that cannot be reasonable avoided or escaped.
9. Ensure good management for any project she/he leads, including effective procedures for promotion of quality and full disclosure of risk.
10. Add to the knowledge of the e-commerce profession by constant study, share the lessons of her/his experience with colleagues, and promote public awareness of benefits and electronic commerce.
11. Conduct herself/himself in the most ethical and competent manner when soliciting professional service or seeking employment, thus meriting confidence in her/his knowledge and integrity.
12. times on all professional assignments without prejudice.
13. Refrain from associating with malicious hackers nor engage in any malicious hacking activities.
14. Not purposefully compromise or cause to be compromised the client organization’s systems in the course of your professional dealings.
15. Ensure all penetration testing activities are authorized and within legal limits.
16. Not partake in any underground hacking communities for purposes of preaching and expanding black hat activities.
