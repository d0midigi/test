# MiTM Overview

### Man-in-the-Middle (MiTM) Attacks Overview

Man-in-the-Middle (MiTM) types of cyberattacks refer to breaches in cybersecurity that make it possible for an adversary to eavesdrop on the data sent back and forth between two people, networks, or computers. It is called a **“man-in-the-middle” attack** because the adversary positions themselves in the “middle” or between the two parties trying to communicate. In effect, the adversary is spying on the interaction between the two parties.

In a MiTM attack, the two parties involved feel like they are communicating as they normally do. What they do not know is that the person actually sending the message illicitly modifies or accesses the message before it reaches its destination. Some ways to protect yourself and your organization from MiTM attacks is by using strong encryption on access points or to use a Virtual Private Network (VPN).
