I'm an Information Security Systems Engineer (ISSE) and ethical hacker and with 20 years experience in the IT field starting out as a network engineer for the DoD in 2004, I was grateful enough to occupy many positions in the cybersecurity umbrella during my 20 tenure with the DoD. I resigned as DoD ISSO in 2019, and have worked in mostly federal industries. I now work in a SCIF as an ISSE for a military agency (NAVY), and so a lot of my current work has me focusing on understanding how attackers think, how they break into systems, and how to stop them with precision and strategy. Part of my job is to protect the networks onboard naval submarines and ships, and this includes making sure their missile and ballistics networks and radars are up to date and what not so as to detect torpedos before they get struck, so a lot of times I find myself having to understand how attackers think, and then I mentally switch gears from "good guy" to "bad guy." I put myself in their shoes...how would they break into these types of systems? If I were them, how would I get in? But the most important part of the focus in knowing how to stop them with precision and strategy.

I am an Information Security Systems Engineer (ISSE) and Ethical Hacker specializing in vulnerability management and analysis, penetration Testing, and both offensive and defensive Active Directory operations. My background includes supporting federal and defense environments, with over 20 years hands-on experience in Cyber Operational Readiness Assessments (CORA) system hardening, incident response, and security architecture.

My work spans across red Team, blue Team, and purple team disciplines. I focus on practical, real-world techniques-ranging from enumeration, privilege escalation, and lateral movement to detection engineering, log analysis, and Cyber defense automation. I love to design training material, write practical guides, and develop repeatable workflows that help others strengthen their security posture and understand adversarial tradecraft.

As a security professional and a mother of three, I value clarity, practicality, and empowerment. This "Cyberepo" exists to teach, demystify, and make cybersecurity accessible while maintaining the professionalism, ethics, and responsibility required in this field and industry. All content here reflects my personal experiences and perspective and does not represent any past, present, or future employer.

I bring experience from vulnerability assessments, penetration Testing, adversary emulation, malware analysis, and secure architecture across DoD and federal environments. I try to blend my approach with real-world tradecraft, MITRE ATT\&CK-aligned methodology, and practical guidance you can apply immediately.

These are my Red Team Field Notes, derived from 17 years of practical hands-on, real-world experience in the field, offering insights into practical tools, techniques, and strategies for conducting successful red team operations and assessments. This compendium includes case studies, POCs, lessons learned, and best practices to help red teamers be as successful as they can be as they embark on their offensive security journey. These notes are heavily influenced by the MITRE ATT\&CK Framework. Readers will discover how to effectively adapt and apply the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) outlined in the Framework to enhance their red teaming capabilities in real-world scenarios.

I aim to create a resource that is accessible to anyone interested in delving into this fascinating area.

I believe that sharing my learning process publicly will not only benefit me in my future projects but also contribute to the growth of the community. Learning something new can be overwhelming, which is why I am simplifying complex concepts through screenshots and videos. My aim is to demystify the subject matter and provide a clear roadmap for beginners by highlighting key terms and search areas to focus on.

I am committed to providing accurate information and illustrating techniques with practical examples. While I am focusing on the basics, I hope to lay a solid foundation for readers to build upon and explore more advanced topics in the future. By breaking down complex information into digestible pieces, I aim to streamline the learning process and facilitate a smoother transition into the world of Red Teaming.

I invite you to join me on this journey of discovery and learning. Your support and feedback are invaluable to me as I continue to refine and expand my knowledge. Together, we can navigate the intricacies of Red Teaming and empower ourselves with the skills and insights needed to excel in this dynamic field. Let's learn, grow, and succeed together.
