What is Hacking? Hacking involves unauthorized access to systems, like logging into an email or remote computer without permission, or accessing restricted information. The term "hacker" originated in the 1960s at MIT during the first known hacking event.

What is Ethical Hacking? Ethical hacking, also known as White hat Hacking or Penetration Testing, involves authorized attempts to access computer systems or data. Ethical hackers use the same tools as malicious hackers but with permission to identify and fix vulnerabilities, thus enhancing system security.

Types of Hacking Hacking can be categorized based on the target:

1. Network Hacking: Gathering information to disrupt network operations.
2. Website Hacking: Unauthorized access to web servers and databases.
3. Computer Hacking: Unauthorized access to computers for information theft.
4. Password Hacking: Recovering stored passwords.
5. Email Hacking: Unauthorized access to email accounts.

Advantages of Hacking

* Recovering lost information, especially passwords.
* Conducting penetration testing to enhance security.
* Assessing network security effectiveness.

Disadvantages of Hacking

* Infringing on privacy.
* Illegality of hacking.
* Potential misuse by criminals and system disruptions.
