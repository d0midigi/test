# Valid Accounts (T1078)

Adversaries may acquire and exploit credentials from existing accounts to achieve Initial Access, Persistence, Privilege Escalation, or Defense Evasion. Compromised credentials can be misused to circumvent access controls on various resources within a system and may provide persistent access to remote systems and external services, such as VPNs, Outlook Web Access, and Remote Desktop. Such credentials may also grant adversaries elevated privileges on specific systems or access to restricted areas of the network. To enhance stealth, adversaries may opt not to use malware or tools in conjunction, making it more challenging to detect their presence.

The risk intensifies when there is an overlap of permissions for local, domain, and cloud accounts across a network of systems. This overlap allows adversaries to pivot through accounts and systems, potentially attaining a high level of access, such as domain or enterprise administrator privileges, to bypass access controls within the enterprise.
