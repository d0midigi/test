# Domain Accounts

Adversaries may acquire and misuse credentials of a domain account to achieve Initial Access, Persistence, Privilege Escalation, or Defense Evasion. Domain accounts, managed by Active Directory Domain Services, encompass users, administrators, and services with configured access and permissions across systems and services within the domain.

Compromising domain accounts, particularly those with elevated privileges, can be accomplished through various methods such as OS Credential Dumping or password reuse. This unauthorized access enables adversaries to exploit privileged resources within the domain.
