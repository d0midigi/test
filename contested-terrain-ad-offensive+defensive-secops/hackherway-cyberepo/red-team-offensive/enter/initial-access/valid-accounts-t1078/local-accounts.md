# Local Accounts

Adversaries may acquire and exploit credentials of local accounts to achieve Initial Access, Persistence, Privilege Escalation, or Defense Evasion. Local accounts are those configured by an organization for user use, remote support, services, or administration on a specific system or service.

Abusing local accounts can also involve elevating privileges and harvesting credentials through OS Credential Dumping techniques. Password reuse may enable adversaries to abuse local accounts across multiple machines on a network, facilitating Privilege Escalation and Lateral Movement.
