# Trusted Relationship (T1199)

Adversaries may exploit organizations with access to their intended victims through trusted party relationships. This approach involves leveraging an existing connection that may not be as protected or scrutinized as standard mechanisms of gaining access to a network.

Organizations frequently grant elevated access to external providers, such as IT services contractors, managed security providers, or infrastructure contractors (e.g., HVAC, elevators, physical security). These relationships may involve third-party access to manage internal systems or cloud-based environments. While access may be intended to be restricted to the infrastructure being maintained, it may exist on the same network as the rest of the enterprise.
