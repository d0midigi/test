# Software Supply Chain Compromise

Adversaries may manipulate application software within the supply chain, aiming to compromise data or systems. This manipulation can occur through various means, such as altering the application's source code, tampering with the update or distribution mechanism, or replacing legitimate software releases with modified versions.

These supply chain compromises may be tailored for specific victims or distributed broadly, with the adversary activating additional tactics only on specific targets. The manipulation of software in the supply chain poses a significant threat, as compromised applications can lead to a range of security issues for end-users.
