# Hardware Supply Chain Compromise

Adversaries can compromise hardware components in products within the supply chain to compromise data or systems. By manipulating hardware or firmware during manufacturing, adversaries can introduce backdoors into consumer networks, providing them with stealthy and pervasive control over the compromised systems. These hardware backdoors may be embedded in a range of devices, including workstations, network infrastructure, or peripherals, making them challenging to detect and presenting a serious threat to the security of affected systems.
