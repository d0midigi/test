# Phishing (T1566)

Attackers may employ the tactic of phishing to gain unauthorized access to targeted systems. Phishing involves the use of electronic communications to deceive individuals into disclosing sensitive information or performing actions that may compromise security. Spearphishing is a more focused form of phishing that targets specific individuals, companies, or industries altogether. In contrast, non-targeted phishing involves casting a wider net, such as through mass email campaigns containing malware or malicious links or attachments.

Phishing attacks often involve sending victims emails with malicious attachments or links designed to execute harmful code on their systems or acquire login credentials. Cybercriminals may also leverage third-party services, such as social media platforms, to conduct phishing campaigns.
