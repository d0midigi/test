# Phishing: Spearphishing Links

T1566.002: Spearphishing Link Adversaries may send spearphishing emails containing malicious links to trick potential victims into clicking on them, leading to the download and execution of malware. This tactic, known as spearphishing with a link, is a specific variant of spearphishing that relies on social engineering techniques to target specific individuals, organizations, or industries.

Unlike other forms of spearphishing that utilize malicious attachments, spearphishing with a link aims to bypass email security defenses that may inspect and block suspicious attachments. In this scenario, the malicious emails include hyperlinks embedded within the body of the message, accompanied by convincing social engineering text to entice the recipient to click on the link. The link may direct the user to a compromised website hosting malware, prompt the download of malicious files, or exploit vulnerabilities in the browser to deploy malware.

Additionally, adversaries may incorporate techniques such as web bugs/web beacons within the links to track email interactions or exploit the end system directly. These malicious links could also lead users to deceptive applications designed to steal sensitive information, such as OAuth tokens, to gain unauthorized access to protected applications and data.

Organizations should educate users about the risks associated with clicking on links provided in unsolicited emails and implement strong email security measures to detect and block spearphishing attempts with malicious links. Continuous monitoring, user training, and endpoint protection solutions can help mitigate the threat posed by spearphishing link attacks.
