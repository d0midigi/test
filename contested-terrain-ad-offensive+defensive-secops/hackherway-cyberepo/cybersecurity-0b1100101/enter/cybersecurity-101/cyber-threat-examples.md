# Cyber Threat Examples

Cybersecurity solutions protect against three main types of cyber threats, which are:

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**Cyber Attack**</mark>**:** Unauthorized information gathering.

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**Cyber Crime**</mark>**:** Groups or single actors targeting systems, networks, or servers for monetary benefit or for causing disruption.

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**Cyber Terrorism**</mark>**:** Undermines electronic systems to cause fear or panic (e.g., mass hysteria).
