# Principles of Cybersecurity

**Establishing a Robust Cybersecurity Program:**

A robust cybersecurity program should adhere to a set of fundamental security principles. Although the implementation of these principles may vary across organizations, the core concepts remain consistent. While each organization may articulate these principles differently, governing cybersecurity policies should encompass a close variant of the following four concepts:

**1. Identifying and Managing Security Risks:** Under the direction of a Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), organizational security risks are identified and managed. This involves:

* Determining and documenting the required confidentiality, integrity, and availability of systems, applications, and information.
* Embedding security risk management processes in applicable risk management frameworks.
* Identifying, documenting, managing, and accepting security risks both before authorization for use and continuously throughout operational life.

**2. Implementing Security Controls to Reduce Risks:** Security controls must be developed, acquired, and applied to protect organizational systems, applications, and information. This includes:

* Designing, deploying, and maintaining systems and applications according to their value.
* Ensuring systems and applications are delivered and supported by trusted suppliers and configured to reduce their attack surface.
* Administering systems and applications securely, addressing vulnerabilities promptly.
* Employing encryption for information at rest and in transit, and controlling, inspecting, and auditing communicated information.
* Backing up information, applications, and configuration settings regularly in a secure manner.
* Granting access to trusted personnel based on need, using multiple methods for identification and authentication.
* Providing ongoing cybersecurity awareness training for personnel.
* Restricting physical access to systems, supporting infrastructure, and facilities to authorized personnel.

**3. Detecting and Understanding Cybersecurity Events:** Swift detection and analysis of security events and anomalous activities are crucial. Utilizing tools such as firewalls, Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS), and Endpoint Detection and Response Systems (EDR) is essential.

**4. Responding to and Recovering from Cybersecurity Incidents:** Given the heightened risk of cyberattacks, being prepared to respond and recover is vital. Policies in this domain should cover:

* Identifying and reporting cybersecurity incidents internally and externally promptly.
* Containing, eradicating, and recovering from cybersecurity incidents immediately.
* Enacting business continuity and disaster recovery plans when required.
