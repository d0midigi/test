# Threats That Challenge Cybersecurity

The methods used by cyber attackers, criminals, or cyber terrorists to gain control over an organization's computer systems or networks include -

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**MALWARE**</mark>

Malware is malicious software. Hackers or cybercriminals create malware to damage or disrupt computer systems or networks. Criminals also use malware to launch cyberattacks or to extract money.

Viruses, Spyware, Trojans, Adware, Botnets, and Ransomware are common instances of malware.

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**PHISHING**</mark>

Phishing targets its victims through authentic-looking bank or company emails, asking for confidential data, such as personal information or credit card details. This is usually done via social engineering techniques, as well (e.g., pretexting, impersonation).

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**MAN-IN-THE-MIDDLE (MiTM) ATTACK**</mark>

In a MiTM attack, internet criminals intercept communications between two individuals for stealing data. Neither party knows the attacker is 'sitting' in the middle between the two parties essentially eavesdropping on the conversation being had. Cybercriminals carry out these attacks mostly on unsecured networks.

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**DENIAL OF SERVICE (DoS) ATTACK**</mark>

This attack overwhelms servers and networks with traffic overload, denying computer systems and/or networks from completing legitimate requests.

**✅️&#x20;**<mark style="color:red;">**SQL INJECTION (SQLi) ATTACK**</mark>

Cybercriminals use a Structured Language Query (SQL) injection to gain access and control databases. This gives attackers access to sensitive data potentially stored in the databases.
