# Organized Cybercrime

#### The Evolution of Organized Crime

In recent years, a significant shift has occurred in the global underworld: the cybercrime industry has surpassed the illegal drug trade as the world’s most profitable illicit enterprise. This massive financial potential has shifted the focus of traditional criminal syndicates toward digital targets.

Modern organized crime groups are characterized by:

* Deep Financial Backing: Unlike individual "script kiddies," these groups are well-funded, allowing them to purchase or develop sophisticated, cutting-edge attack tools.
* High Motivation: They operate like a business. If a technique can be monetized—whether through data theft, corporate espionage, or ransomware—they will invest the resources to execute it.
* Professionalism: These entities often function with a corporate-like structure, employing specialists to handle different stages of an attack, from initial breach to money laundering.

> Key Takeaway: As an ethical hacker, you are often defending against an adversary that has the budget and patience of a mid-sized corporation, driven entirely by the pursuit of profit.
