In this book, my ultimate goal is to provide you with a structured, practice-oriented understanding of how adversaries identify, exploit, and maintain persistent access within Active Directory-based domain and underlying network infrastructures. Drawing on almost two decades of experience across offensive and defensive security, incident response, and defensive architecture, the material is grounded in real-world techniques, case studies, and hard-won lessons from working alongside highly skilled ethical hackers and red and blue teams.

You will also learn how to systematically evaluate an Active Directory environment for security weaknesses, including misconfigurations, privilege escalation and defined attack pathways, and trust relationships that attackers routinely abuse. In parallel, you will study defensive strategies, security controls and remediate those same attack pathways before they are maliciously leveraged in a real-world breach attempt or full-blown incident.

More specifically, this book aims to guide you through the following concept that are presented here within:

* Understanding the internal mechanics of Active Directory and common enterprise deployment patterns
* Identiying nd exploiting typical AD weaknesses as an atacker (reconnaissance, latral movement, credential theft, and persistence)
* Designing and implementing hardening measures, least-privilege models, and network segmentation for AD environments
* Detecting to and responding to AD-centric attacks using logging, threat hunting, and security tooling
* Integrating offensive insights into defensive engineering, policy, and operational procedures

The overarching goal is to help you adopt an informed adversarial mindset so that you can think as an attacker while, at the same time, acting as a defender. By the end of this book, you should not only understand how attacks against Active Directory are planned and executed, but also be able to design, implement, and maintain resilient defenses that can withstand current, emerging, and sustained offensive campaigns.
