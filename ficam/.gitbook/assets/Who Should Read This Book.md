Read this book if you work in the information security field, or have a profound interest in any of the many security domains available within the cybersecurity umbrella. Readers should have a pretty good idea of how t use a computer, and ideally have some experience installing an operating system, and various applications and programs. The material contained within this book is aimed at mid- to advanced level; however, i do my best to provide some of the most basic security fundamentals that both novice and expert professionals and practitioners need to understand before moving forward onto other complex topics. Many topics in cybersecurity branch off of some underlying technology or topology so knowing or being familiar with those first sets the ultimate groundwork for security hacking and defense.

If you are a beginning information or cybersecurity practitioner, you may struggle a bit with some of the material presented here within,. Furthermore, there are some beginner techniques that are explained and demonstrated, such as diffing, which will serve the learning reader through all levels of the skills that must be acquired before moving forward.
