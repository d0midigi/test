### Abstract

Federal identity security operates as an interconnected trust architecture comprising authoritative identity sources, sponsors, credential issuers, identity providers, enterprise directories, Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), federation services, access control systems, platforms, and administrative authorities. Chapter 3 outlines the foundational architecture for Federal Identity, Credential, and Access Management (FICAM) and Department of Defense (DoD) Identity, Credential, and Management (ICAM) frameworks.
