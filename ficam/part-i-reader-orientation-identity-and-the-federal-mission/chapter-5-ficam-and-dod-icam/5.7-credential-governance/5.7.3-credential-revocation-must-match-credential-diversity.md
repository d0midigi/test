# 5.7.3 Credential Revocation Must Match Credential Diversity

Revocation also becomes more difficult when identities possess multiple authenticators.

A user's password can be reset without revoking an authentication certificate.

A certificate can be revoked without terminating an existing cloud session.

An active token can remain useful after another authenticator is removed.

A lost device may contain protected credential material that needs separate treatment from the user's PIV.

The incident responder must therefore know what authentication material actually exists.

This requirement seems obvious until a real incident occurs.

Identity inventories frequently tell responders which accounts exist.

They do not necessarily reveal every credential, certificate, token, application authorization, device registration, or session through which those accounts can still act.

Credential governance must make the authenticator estate visible enough that revocation is possible.

Otherwise, containment becomes guesswork.
