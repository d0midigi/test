# 5.11.6 Workload Identity Changes the Scale of Identity Governance

Cloud and automation architectures introduce identities that can be created and destroyed much more rapidly than traditional enterprise accounts.

A workload may exist for minutes.

An application deployment may create several service identities automatically.

Infrastructure-as-code can reproduce the same identity pattern across many environments.

This changes the scale at which governance must operate.

A quarterly manual review is poorly suited to an identity population that changes continuously.

The controls must increasingly be designed into the provisioning mechanism itself.

Which workload is allowed to create an identity?

What authority does the new identity receive by default?

How is its credential generated?

Can the credential be exported?

What terminates the identity when the workload disappears?

How is unused authority identified?

The security architecture has to move from reviewing identities after creation to constraining how identities are created.

That is a major shift.

Identity governance becomes part of platform engineering.
