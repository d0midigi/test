# 5.11.1 Person Identity Has a Natural Authoritative Relationship

Human identities generally have an identifiable real-world relationship that provides a starting point for governance.

The person is employed.

The person serves in a military capacity.

The person is a contractor.

The person is sponsored as a mission partner.

The relationship has an owner and eventually ends.

That does not make human identity easy to govern, but it provides an authoritative anchor.

The enterprise can ask whether the person is still employed, whether the contract remains active, whether the mission assignment changed, or whether sponsorship still exists.

Those events can drive lifecycle decisions.

An NPE may have no equivalent natural event unless one is deliberately created.

A service account remains active because the service still runs.

The service still runs because no one has retired the application.

The application has not been retired because no owner has accepted responsibility for determining whether it is still required.

The identity therefore survives not because anyone confirmed it should exist, but because nothing forced it to disappear.

That is a weaker lifecycle model.
