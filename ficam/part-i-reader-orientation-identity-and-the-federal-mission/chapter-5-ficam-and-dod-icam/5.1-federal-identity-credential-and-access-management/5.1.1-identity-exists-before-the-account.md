# 5.1.1 Identity Exists Before the Account

For a federal workforce identity, the digital account normally exists because some prior process established that an individual has a legitimate relationship with the agency or command. That relationship may be connected to employment, military service, contracting, sponsorship, mission participation, or another recognized basis for access.

The account is a representation of that relationship.

This becomes important when the source relationship changes.

If an employee separates, the security objective is not merely to disable one Active Directory account. Every identity representation and access dependency associated with the person should eventually reflect the change. A locally managed directory object, a cloud identity, a privileged account, a certificate, a sponsored external identity, an application-specific account, and physical-access privileges may all require coordinated lifecycle action.

This is why identity lifecycle management is broader than Active Directory account management.

The directory can faithfully enforce whatever identity state it has been given while still being wrong from the enterprise perspective.

A user object may be technically valid and operationally obsolete.
