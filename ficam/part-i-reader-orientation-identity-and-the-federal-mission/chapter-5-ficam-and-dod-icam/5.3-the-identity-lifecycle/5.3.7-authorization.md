# 5.3.7 Authorization

Authorization converts authenticated identity into permitted action.

In federal ICAM, this may be expressed through roles, attributes, group memberships, entitlements, access control entries, application policy, or combinations of several mechanisms.

The authorization system should consume identity state without confusing identity with permission.

A user's verified identity does not inherently entitle that user to a resource.

A CAC does not contain a universal right to access every DoD system.

An enterprise identifier does not grant authority simply because it is unique.

The relying resource must determine whether the authenticated principal possesses an appropriate relationship to the requested resource.

This is where local mission policy remains important even in highly centralized identity architectures.

Enterprise ICAM can establish common identity.

Authorization remains resource-specific.
