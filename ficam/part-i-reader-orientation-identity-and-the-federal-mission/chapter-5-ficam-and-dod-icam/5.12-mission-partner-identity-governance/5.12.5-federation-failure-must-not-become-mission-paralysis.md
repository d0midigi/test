# 5.12.5 Federation Failure Must Not Become Mission Paralysis

There is also an availability side to mission-partner identity.

A highly centralized federation architecture can simplify access dramatically while creating dependency on services outside the local environment.

If the external identity provider becomes unavailable, can the mission continue?

If network connectivity between partners is interrupted, do already established sessions continue?

Can emergency or degraded operations occur without creating uncontrolled local accounts that later become permanent?

The correct answer varies by mission.

The important point is that the failure behavior should be designed rather than improvised.

Some environments may require access to fail closed immediately.

Others may support limited continuity based on previously established trust.

Tactical environments may require locally provisioned identities because upstream federation is unavailable for extended periods.

The security architecture has to balance current identity freshness against mission continuity consciously.

Degraded operations should represent an alternate controlled state, not abandonment of ICAM.
