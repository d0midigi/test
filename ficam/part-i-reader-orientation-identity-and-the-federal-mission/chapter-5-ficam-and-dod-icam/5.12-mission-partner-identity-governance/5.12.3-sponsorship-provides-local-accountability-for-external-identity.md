# 5.12.3 Sponsorship Provides Local Accountability for External Identity

Federation can eliminate the need to administer the partner's credential.

It does not eliminate the need for local accountability.

An external identity should have a local reason for access.

Sponsorship provides one mechanism for preserving that reason.

The sponsor represents the local mission relationship rather than the external identity itself. The partner organization can remain authoritative for the person's identity while the sponsor remains accountable for why the local environment is accepting that identity.

This becomes particularly important when missions change.

The external person may remain legitimately employed by the partner organization long after the local mission requirement ends.

The partner identity should therefore remain active at its source.

Local authorization should not.

Without sponsorship or equivalent governance, those two lifecycle events can become incorrectly coupled. The relying environment continues granting access simply because the external identity continues to authenticate successfully.

Valid identity becomes mistaken for continuing need.
