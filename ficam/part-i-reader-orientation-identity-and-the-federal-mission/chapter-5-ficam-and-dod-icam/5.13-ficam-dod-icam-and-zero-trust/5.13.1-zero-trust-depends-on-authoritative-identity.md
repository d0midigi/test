# 5.13.1 Zero Trust Depends on Authoritative Identity

A continuous access decision cannot be better than the identity data used to make it.

If an account should have been disabled but remains active, continuous evaluation will repeatedly evaluate a stale identity.

If the user's group membership is excessive, the policy engine may consistently authorize excessive access.

If a device-compliance attribute is wrong, the device may be trusted under false conditions.

If a privileged role remains assigned after the mission requirement ends, adaptive access does not correct the entitlement.

Zero Trust can make access decisions more contextual and more frequent.

It cannot make corrupted identity state true.

This is why identity lifecycle and attribute governance are foundational to Zero Trust architecture.

The policy decision point consumes identity state.

FICAM helps determine whether that state deserves to be consumed.
