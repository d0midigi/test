# 5.11 Department of Defense Issuances and Identity Security

## 5.11 Department of Defense Issuances and Identity Security

### 5.11.1 Department of Defense Instructions

{% hint style="info" %}
Department of Defense Instructions (DoDIs) establish mandatory policy and responsibilities across a broad range of information-security and identity functions.
{% endhint %}

### 5.11.2 The DoD 8520-Series Identity Policy Family

{% hint style="info" %}
DoD identity-related issuances within the 8520 policy family establish requirements for public-key infrastructure, identity authentication, and credential usage across defense systems.
{% endhint %}

### 5.11.3 Policy Must Be Mapped to Technical Behavior

{% hint style="info" %}
Practitioners should be able to identify which authentication methods, certificate mappings, credential stores, identity providers, or application configurations implement a policy requirement.
{% endhint %}

### 5.11.4 Policy Exceptions Create Technical Debt

{% hint style="info" %}
Approved exceptions may be operationally necessary, but they must remain visible, time-bound where appropriate, risk-assessed, and supported by compensating protections.
{% endhint %}
