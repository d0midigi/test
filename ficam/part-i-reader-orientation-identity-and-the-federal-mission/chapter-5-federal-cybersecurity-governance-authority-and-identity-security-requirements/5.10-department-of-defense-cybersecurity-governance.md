# 5.10 Department of Defense Cybersecurity Governance

## 5.10 Department of Defense Cybersecurity Governance

### 5.10.1 Department of Defense Chief Information Officer

{% hint style="info" %}
The Department of Defense Chief Information Officer (DoD CIO) establishes enterprise-level cybersecurity, information-technology, cloud, identity, and digital-modernization direction.
{% endhint %}

### 5.10.2 Defense Information Systems Agency

{% hint style="info" %}
The Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) provides enterprise services, technical implementation guidance, security requirements, network infrastructure, and operational support across the Department of Defense.
{% endhint %}

### 5.10.3 National Security Agency

{% hint style="info" %}
The National Security Agency (NSA) contributes technical cybersecurity guidance, cryptographic expertise, intelligence, hardening recommendations, and National Security System security capabilities.
{% endhint %}

### 5.10.4 United States Cyber Command

{% hint style="info" %}
United States Cyber Command (USCYBERCOM) conducts cyberspace operations and contributes operational direction affecting defense of the Department of Defense Information Network.
{% endhint %}

### 5.10.5 Joint Force Headquarters-Department of Defense Information Network

{% hint style="info" %}
Joint Force Headquarters-Department of Defense Information Network (JFHQ-DODIN) directs defensive cyberspace operations and helps coordinate protection of the Department of Defense Information Network.
{% endhint %}

### 5.10.6 Military Departments and Service Components

{% hint style="info" %}
The Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Space Force, and supporting components issue subordinate policy, technical guidance, operational direction, and implementation requirements.
{% endhint %}

### 5.10.7 Combatant Commands

{% hint style="info" %}
Combatant commands operate mission environments whose identity-security requirements must align enterprise governance with operational realities.
{% endhint %}
