### 5.23.1 From Cybersecurity Governance to FICAM and DoD ICAM Architecture

{% hint style="info" %}

Chapter 6 narrows the focus to the identity discipline itself: Federal Identity, Credential, and Access Management, Department of Defense ICAM, identity proofing, credentialing, assurance levels, authoritative attributes, federation, person and non-person entities, and the identity lifecycle.

{% hint style="info" %}
