# 3.8 Contractor-Operated and Shared Infrastructure

Contractors are integral to federal and Department of Defense technology operations. They may administer systems, develop applications, operate security platforms, provide cloud services, maintain infrastructure, support mission programs, or perform functions indistinguishable at the keyboard from those performed by government personnel.

The relevant identity-security issue is therefore not whether an administrator is government or contractor personnel.

It is how authority is established, governed, monitored, and revoked.

A contractor administrator who controls a Tier 0 platform possesses Tier 0-relevant authority regardless of employment category.

The security model must follow the capability.
