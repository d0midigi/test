# 3.7.1 Federation Preserves Administrative Independence

The principal advantage of federation is that trust can be extended without collapsing administrative boundaries.

A partner remains responsible for authenticating its own users.

The relying environment remains responsible for deciding what those authenticated users are permitted to access.

This separation is powerful because neither side must become the complete administrator of the other.

It also means security responsibility is shared.

The identity provider must protect its authentication systems and signing authority.

The relying environment must constrain which assertions it accepts and how those assertions become authorization.

Claims transformation, attribute mapping, group assignment, entitlement policy, and application configuration become part of the trust relationship.

The cryptographic validity of the assertion is necessary.

It is not sufficient.

The relying environment must still determine whether the assertion should result in the requested authority.
