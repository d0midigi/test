# 3.9.2 Recovery Dependencies Must Be Identified Separately

Normal-operation dependencies and recovery dependencies are not necessarily the same.

During normal operations, administrators may authenticate through a privileged-access platform.

During forest recovery, that platform may be unavailable.

During normal operations, administrative documentation may reside on a collaboration system.

During an identity outage, access to that system may depend on the identity infrastructure being recovered.

During normal operations, backup administrators may use domain accounts.

During a domain compromise, those same accounts may no longer be trusted.

Recovery architecture must therefore preserve an independent path to the capabilities required to restore identity.

That may include isolated credentials, protected documentation, clean administrative workstations, trusted backups, offline recovery procedures, and access to critical cryptographic or configuration material.

The exact implementation varies by environment.

The architectural principle does not:

The systems required to restore trust cannot depend completely on the trust that has just failed.
