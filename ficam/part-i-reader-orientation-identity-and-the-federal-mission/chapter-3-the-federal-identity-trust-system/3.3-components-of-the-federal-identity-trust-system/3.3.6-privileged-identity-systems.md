# 3.3.6 Privileged Identity Systems

Privileged Access Management (PAM), Privileged Identity Management (PIM), credential vaults, session brokers, Just-in-Time access platforms, and related privileged-access systems exist to reduce the risks associated with standing administrative authority.

Because they mediate privilege, they become high-value identity systems themselves.

A privileged-access vault that stores administrative credentials is an obvious target.

A platform capable of granting temporary privileged role membership may be equally valuable even if it stores no reusable password.

A session-brokering system may control which administrator can reach which Tier 0 resource.

An approval system may determine when dormant privilege becomes active.

These platforms should therefore not be evaluated only according to whether they protect privileged credentials.

Their own administrative and recovery paths must be protected according to the authority they can grant.

A security control that mediates Tier 0 access can itself become part of Tier 0.
