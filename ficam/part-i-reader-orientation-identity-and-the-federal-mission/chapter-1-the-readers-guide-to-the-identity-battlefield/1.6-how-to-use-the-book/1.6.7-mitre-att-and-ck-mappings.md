# 1.6.7 MITRE ATT\&CK Mappings

The MITRE ATT\&CK adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures (TTP) knowledgebase matrix is used throughout the offensive material as a common vocabulary for adversarial behavior. The mappings identify where a technique aligns with established TTPs, but the framework does not replace the technical explanation.

That distinction is also particularly important to note because in Active Directory, one identity operation may support several stages of an intrusion depending on how it is used. Credential theft may enable initial access in one scenario and lateral movement in another. A certificate may function as a credential, an escalation mechanism, or a persistence mechanism. Directory replication can be used to obtain credential material, but the strategic consequence may be domain dominance.

The ATT\&CK mapping therefore accompanies the analysis rather than dictating its boundaries. Readers should use the mappings to connect the book's identity-centric model to the broader adversary-behavior framework while retaining the more detailed architectural reasoning developed here.
