# 1.6 How to Use the Book

Contested Terrain is designed to be read sequentially the first time through, but it is not meant to become a book that can only be used from beginning to end. The early chapters establish dependencies that later chapters assume, while the middle and later portions are intentionally structured so they can function as operational references after the reader has completed the foundational material.

That distinction is important to note because the way a reader uses the book will change over time. On a first reading, Chapter 13 may be where Kerberos finally becomes coherent as an authentication systems rather than a collection of ticket-related terminology. Months later, during an assessment, that same chapter may be reopened simply to verify the relationship between Service Principal Names (SPNs), service tickets, delegation, and the Privilege Attribute Certificate (PAC). During an incident, the recovery material may become more important than the offensive chapter that originally introduced the compromise mechanism.

The objective is therefore twofold: teach the identity system progressively, then leave behind a reference architecture that remains useful after the instructional sequence has been completed.
