# 1.2.6 Ethical Hackers, Penetration Testers, Red Teams, and Purple Teams

The offensive reader will find extensive coverage of enumeration, credential attacks, authorization abuse, delegation, certificate services, hybrid identity, lateral movement, persistence, domain dominance, and related tradecraft.

But exploitation is not the end state of the analysis.

A finding becomes substantially more valuable when the operator can explain the trust failure that enabled it in the first place, the evidence it produces, the defensive control that should interrupt it, and the operational consequence of successful exploitation.

That is particularly important in federal environments, where technical findings must often be translated into risk, authorization, remediation, and mission terms.
