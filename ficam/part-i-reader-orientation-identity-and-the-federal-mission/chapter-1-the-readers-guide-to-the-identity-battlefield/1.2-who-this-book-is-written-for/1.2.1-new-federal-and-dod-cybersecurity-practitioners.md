# 1.2.1 New Federal and DoD Cybersecurity Practitioners

A new practitioner entering federal cybersecurity encounters a vocabulary problem almost immediately.

Active Directory terminology intersects with networking terminology. Networking intersects with authentication. Authentication intersects with PKI. PKI intersects with PIV and CAC. Those mechanisms sit beneath policy, governance, assessment, authorization, vulnerability management, and mission requirements.

The result can make a first federal cybersecurity position feel as though everyone else was handed a dictionary that the new employee somehow missed.

This book is designed to eliminate that problem.

Concepts are introduced before their abuse is demonstrated. Protocol mechanics precede protocol exploitation. Architecture precedes attack-pathway analysis. Federal governance is connected directly to the technical systems it influences rather than discussed in isolation.

A reader should not need twenty years of federal experience to understand why a finding matters.

This book will build that context.
