# 1.2.7 Incident Responders and Recovery + Triage Engineers

Identity compromise creates a difficult triage and recovery problem because restoring computers is not necessarily the same thing as restoring trust.

A rebuilt workstation does not invalidate a forged Kerberos ticket.

A password reset does not necessarily invalidate a stolen certificate.

Removing malware does not remove an ACL or RAT backdoor.

Reimaging endpoints does not restore a compromised certificate authority.

Disabling a user does not necessarily terminate every cloud session associated with that identity.

Later chapters therefore treat recovery as an identity engineering discipline in its own right.

The question after a major compromise is not merely:

_"Did we remove the attacker?"_

It is:

_"Can we once again trust the mechanisms that tell us who the attacker is?"_

That distinction is fundamental to the rest of this book.
