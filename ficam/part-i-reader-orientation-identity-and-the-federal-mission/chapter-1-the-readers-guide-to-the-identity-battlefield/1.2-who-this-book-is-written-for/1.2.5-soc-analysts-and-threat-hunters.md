# 1.2.5 SOC Analysts and Threat Hunters

Identity attacks frequently resemble legitimate network and administrative activity.

Kerberos tickets are supposed to be requested.

LDAP is supposed to query the directory multiple times a day.

Administrators are supposed to modify groups.

Domain Controllers are supposed to replicate every 90 minutes.

Certificates are supposed to go through the enrollment process before issuance.

Remote administration is supposed to occur for the userbase.

Detection therefore depends heavily on context.

Later chapters examine not only which telemetry exists, but why particular combinations of identity events matter and how an analyst can distinguish expected administration from adversarial use of legitimate mechanisms.
