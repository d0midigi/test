# 1.2.2 Systems and Active Directory Administrators

Administrators already understand much of the terrain operationally.

They know which Domain Controllers are temperamental.

They know which Group Policy changes create support calls.

They know which service account nobody wants to touch.

They know which legacy application still requires an authentication mechanism everyone wishes could be disabled.

What administrators may not always see is how those operational exceptions appear form an adversarial perspective.

This book takes familiar administrative objects and relationships and examines their security consequences.

The objective is not to turn administrators into penetration testers.

It is to make attack-pathway reasoning part of competent administration.
