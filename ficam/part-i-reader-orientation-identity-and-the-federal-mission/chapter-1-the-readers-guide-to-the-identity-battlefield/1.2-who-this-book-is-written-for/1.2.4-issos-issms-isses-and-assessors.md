# 1.2.4 ISSOs, ISSMs, ISSEs, and Assessors

Federal security practitioners are often placed between policy and engineering.

A requirement may say what must be protected without explaining precisely how an adversary would exploit a failure.

A vulnerability scanner may report a weakness without explaining whether it participates in a meaningful identity attack pathway.

A technical team may describe a configuration as low risk because it does not grant obvious administrative privilege.

This book provides the technical context required to challenge those assumptions.

The objective is not merely to produce better compliance evidence.

It is to produce evidence that corresponds to actual security state.
