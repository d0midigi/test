# 1.2.3 Identity and ICAM Engineers

Identity practitioners frequently operate across technologies that traditional Active Directory material treats separately: directories, credentials, certificates, authentication assurance, federation, attributes, cloud identity, provisioning, privileged access, and lifecycle governance.

This book treats those systems as one interconnected trust environment.

For the ICAM engineer, the offensive material provides a means of validating whether the intended trust architecture survives contact with an adversary.
