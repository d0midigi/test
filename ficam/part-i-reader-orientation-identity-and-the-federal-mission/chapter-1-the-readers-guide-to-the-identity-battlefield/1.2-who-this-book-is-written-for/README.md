# 1.2 Who This Book Is Written For

This book is deliberately written for more than one job title.

The intended reader may administer Active Directory every day, may have never touched a Domain Controller, may spend most of the workweek inside eMASS, may live in a SIEM console, may conduct penetration tests, or may be entering their first federal cybersecurity position.

The requirement is not that every reader begin at the same level.

The requirement is that every reader eventually understand how their discipline connects to the others.
