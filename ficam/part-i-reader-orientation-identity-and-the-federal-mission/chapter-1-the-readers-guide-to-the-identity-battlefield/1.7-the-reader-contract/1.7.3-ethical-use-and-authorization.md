# 1.7.3 Ethical Use and Authorization

The offensive material in this book exists for authorized security assessment, adversary emulation, laboratory education, defensive validation, and security research conducted within appropriate legal and operational boundaries.

Identity systems deserve particular caution because their reach is rarely limited to the system immediately being tested. Centralized authentication, Group Policy, certificates, federation, synchronization, and privileged management infrastructure can create consequences well beyond the apparent target.

For that reason, authorization is not treated as a legal paragraph placed at the beginning of the book and forgotten. Operational boundaries return throughout the technical material when a technique introduces meaningful risk.

A tester who understands the exploit but cannot judge when its execution is unsafe is not fully competent in the technique.
