# 1.7.5 Production-System Safety

Production identity infrastructure should be approached with the assumption that undocumented dependencies exist.

This is one of the most useful operating assumptions a practitioner can carry into a mature Active Directory environment.

An account that appears unused may run a scheduled process once each month. A certificate may support an application whose owner left years ago. An inherited permission may exist because removing it once caused an outage nobody documented. A trust may support a mission partner known only to another team. A Group Policy Object with an obsolete name may still contain a security setting essential to a legacy system.

None of those possibilities justify preserving unsafe configurations indefinitely. They do mean that remediation should be engineered rather than improvised.

Good security work reduces risk without discovering hidden dependencies through avoidable outages.
