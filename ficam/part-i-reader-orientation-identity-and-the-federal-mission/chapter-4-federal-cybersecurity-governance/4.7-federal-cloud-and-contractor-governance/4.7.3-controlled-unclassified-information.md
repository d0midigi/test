# 4.7.3 Controlled Unclassified Information

Controlled Unclassified Information demonstrates why federal security requirements can extend into nonfederal systems.

NIST SP 800-171 Revision 3 provides recommended security requirements for protecting the confidentiality of CUI when that information resides in nonfederal systems and organizations. The associated SP 800-171A provides assessment procedures for evaluating those requirements.

For defense contractors, DFARS requirements further establish contractual cybersecurity obligations for covered contractor information systems processing covered defense information, including requirements tied to NIST SP 800-171 and cyber incident reporting.

The identity significance is immediate.

CUI protection depends on knowing who may access the information.

That requires account governance, authentication, authorization, least privilege, auditability, remote-access control, privileged administration, and reliable removal of access.

A contractor can encrypt every database containing CUI and still fail to protect the information if identities with excessive authority can decrypt it legitimately.

The identity plane determines who is treated as an authorized user of the protective mechanisms.
