# 4.8.4 Security Controls

Security controls convert broad requirements into implementable security capabilities.

NIST SP 800-53 is central to that layer because it provides a catalog through which security and privacy requirements can be selected, tailored, implemented, and assessed according to the applicable risk-management process.

Identity engineering enters deeply at this point.

A control requiring account management becomes provisioning, review, disabling, and lifecycle operations.

A control requiring least privilege becomes actual group and permission architecture.

A control requiring separation of duties becomes administrative-role design.

A control requiring authenticator management becomes credential issuance, reset, revocation, and recovery.

A control requiring audit becomes telemetry.

A control requiring contingency capability becomes identity recovery.

The control is no longer conceptual once the system implements it.
