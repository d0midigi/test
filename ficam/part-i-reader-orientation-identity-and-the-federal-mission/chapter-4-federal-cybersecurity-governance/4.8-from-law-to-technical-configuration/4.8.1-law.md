# 4.8.1 Law

Law establishes the highest-level obligation in this chain.

FISMA, for example, requires federal agencies to operate information-security programs and manage security risk. It does not instruct an Active Directory engineer to enable a particular audit subcategory or remove a particular access control entry.

The absence of technical detail is intentional.

Legislation must remain meaningful across technologies that may not exist when the law is enacted.

Its function is to establish responsibility and expected outcome.
