# 4.8.2 Policy

Policy interprets that obligation within an executive, departmental, agency, or mission context.

Policy begins narrowing the problem.

It identifies who is responsible, which systems are affected, what objectives must be met, what reporting or assessment processes apply, and what organizational risk decisions are permitted.

For identity security, policy may establish requirements surrounding privileged access, digital identity, authentication, credentialing, Zero Trust, cloud use, mission-partner access, or system authorization.

The policy still should not be mistaken for the implementation.

It establishes the rules under which implementation occurs.
