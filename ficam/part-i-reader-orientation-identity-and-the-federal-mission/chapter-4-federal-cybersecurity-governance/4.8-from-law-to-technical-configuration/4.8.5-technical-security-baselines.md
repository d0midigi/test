# 4.8.5 Technical Security Baselines

Technical security baselines provide the configuration specificity that higher-level controls intentionally lack.

In DoD environments, STIGs and SRGs are among the most important examples. DISA identifies these resources as standardized technical security guidance that bridges broader NIST and RMF security requirements into DoD implementation.

This layer is where governance becomes recognizable to administrators.

A password-policy setting.

A user right.

An LDAP protection requirement.

An audit configuration.

A service restriction.

A registry value.

A certificate configuration.

These settings are measurable and testable.

They are also where compliance can become dangerously narrow if the practitioner forgets the layers above.

The setting exists to achieve a security outcome.

Passing the check is evidence that the setting is correct.

It is not proof that the complete outcome exists.
