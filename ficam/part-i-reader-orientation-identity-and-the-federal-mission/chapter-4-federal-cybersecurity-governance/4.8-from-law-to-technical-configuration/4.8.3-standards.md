# 4.8.3 Standards

Standards create common technical or risk-management expectations.

FIPS 199 provides a common categorization method.

FIPS 200 establishes minimum security requirements.

FIPS 201 establishes PIV requirements.

Other standards establish cryptographic, authentication, interoperability, or security expectations relevant to identity infrastructure.

Standards matter because independently designed security controls become difficult to compare and govern.

A shared standard creates a stable reference point.
