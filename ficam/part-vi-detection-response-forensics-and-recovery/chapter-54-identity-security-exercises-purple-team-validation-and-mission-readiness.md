# Chapter 54 - Identity Security Exercises, Purple-Team Validation, and Mission Readiness

### Abstract

Identity security must be exercised under realistic operational conditions before an actual compromise forces the agency or command to discover whether its architecture, personnel, telemetry, containment procedures, recovery mechanisms, and mission dependencies work as intended. This chapter examines identity-focused red-team exercises, purple-team validation, tabletop exercises, cyber ranges, breach-and-attack simulation, attack-path emulation, control testing, detection validation, recovery exercises, degraded-operation scenarios, and cross-boundary coordination. Offensive analysis focuses on constructing realistic identity attack sequences that test authentication, credential exposure, privilege escalation, lateral movement, persistence, control-plane compromise, hybrid authority, PKI, and mission impact without creating unacceptable operational risk. Defensive analysis emphasizes observable evidence, detection coverage, containment effectiveness, recovery performance, attack-path reduction, corrective action, and repeatable readiness measurement. The chapter establishes that readiness is not demonstrated by possessing procedures or tools; it is demonstrated when personnel and controls can detect, contain, reconstruct, and recover from realistic identity compromise while preserving mission capability.
