**Crucial Concepts Missing to Consider**

#### **1. Crypto-Agility and Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) Readiness**

Upgrading Active Directory PKI certificate authorities (AD CS), Kerberos encryption primitives, and TLS/LDAPS trust chains to integratte FIPS-approved post-quantum algorithms (such as ML-KEM for key encapsulation and ML-DSA for digital signatures) to prevent "harvest now, decrypt later, intelligence operations.

#### 2. Identity Threat Detection and Response (ITDR) Convergence

Bridging the architectural chasm between static Identity and Access Management (IAM) provisioning and real-time ITDR telemetry by continuously calculating effective permissions, detecting shadow admin pathways, and analyzing protocol-level control plane anomalies across hybrid enclaves.

#### 3. Machine-Identity Exposure and Non-Human Governance

Establishing dedicated lifecycle governance, automated credential rotation, and anomalous API behavior monitoring for non-human identities (service principals, managed identities, and OAuth applications) which represent the fastest-growing enterprise attack surface.
