# 3.4 Stay Current with Vendor-Supported Hardware

Staying current with vendor-supported hardware is essential to ensure the stability, security, and reliability of network devices. Vendors eventually discontinue support for specific hardware platforms, which can lead to various risks and challenges.

End-of-life hardware devices pose several concerns, including device instability, increased memory requirements, and a higher risk of exploitation by adversaries due to unaddressed vulnerabilities. Newer hardware platforms supported by vendors offer improved security features and protection against known vulnerabilities, making them more resilient to attacks.

When a vendor announces the end-of-life for a device or discontinues support, it is crucial to develop a plan to upgrade or replace the affected devices with newer equipment as per vendor recommendations. Prompt action should be taken to upgrade or replace outdated or unsupported devices to maintain the availability of network services and ensure ongoing security support. Delaying upgrades or replacements can leave the network vulnerable to potential threats and compromises.
